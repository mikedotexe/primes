# Hardened Research Programs

**Updated**: April 2026
**Purpose**: stable collaborator-facing language for the repo's five strongest
live research programs

This note is a dual-register surface:

- `Maintained statement` is the wording that should stay stable across the repo
  unless the evidence changes.
- `Constructive steelman` preserves the strongest plausible intuitive framing
  without promoting it to claim status.

These entries are not new claims. They are disciplined research frames tied to
the current evidence surface in [`CLAIMS.md`](../CLAIMS.md),
[`VERIFIED_FACTS_VS_SPECULATION.md`](../VERIFIED_FACTS_VS_SPECULATION.md), and
the maintained report/examples layer.

## Current Signal Ladder

This is not a novelty ranking and not a theorem ranking. It is a practical map
of where the live signal currently seems strongest, what kind of signal it is,
and what is bottlenecking further progress.

| Rank | Program | Signal role | Why it still looks alive | Main bottleneck |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | bounded-`k` threshold / compactness regime | primary arithmetic signal | It keeps surviving controls, cross-base checks, decomposition, and formal exact transfer framing. | Turning the maintained `M=1 → 2 → 3` shape into an explicit theorem or reduction. |
| 2 | cross-base hinge / species classification | explanatory signal | It narrows the threshold lane into distinct species instead of one vague anomaly cloud. | Isolating the exact discriminator that separates hinge-like overlap survival from boundary-release cases. |
| 3 | connector directional asymmetry | narrow residual signal | Some corrected connector families still retain directional structure after residue and density controls. | Escaping the canonical-pair bottleneck and finding a smaller cross-family classifier. |
| 4 | formal exact substrate | rigor multiplier | It keeps shrinking ambiguity by proving the exact finite grammar beneath the empirical story. | Translating more of the report language into exact finite statements without smuggling prose labels into the proofs. |
| 5 | geometry / visual lane as discovery surface | discovery signal | The plots are increasingly helping classify the solution space instead of merely illustrating it. | Repeatedly back-translating visual patterns into arithmetic features that survive quantitative checks. |

## How To Read The Ladder

- Higher rank means stronger current leverage on the repo's live mathematical
  frontier, not greater public claim strength.
- A lower-ranked lane can still be very important if it is the best discovery
  aid or rigor aid for the stronger lanes above it.
- If a lane moves up, it should be because a bottleneck was removed, not
  because the prose became more ambitious.

## 1. Bounded-`k` Threshold / Compactness Regime

**Maintained statement**
The strongest live arithmetic signal in the repo is the bounded-`k` transition
lane: anomaly-rich behavior at `M=1`, a sparse boundary layer at `M=2`, and a
compact stable regime by `M=3` on the maintained catalog.

**Constructive steelman**
If one wants to keep some of the old chaos/stability intuition alive, the most
defensible translation is not dynamical chaos but a short-length arithmetic
threshold. The poetic core that survives is that compactness eventually
overwhelms local obstruction effects, and `M=2` is the last place where a
small ecology of exceptions can still breathe.

**What we should not say yet**
We should not state a general theorem that `k=(0,0)` wins for every base,
family, or catalog once `M>=3`, and we should not treat the threshold story as
a density theorem or a dynamical-systems result.

**Current evidence anchors**

- [`examples/m_transition_curve_report.rs`](../examples/m_transition_curve_report.rs)
- [`examples/m2_m3_transition_report.rs`](../examples/m2_m3_transition_report.rs)
- [`examples/chaos_threshold_translation_report.rs`](../examples/chaos_threshold_translation_report.rs)
- [`collab/CHAOS_THRESHOLD_TRANSLATION.md`](./CHAOS_THRESHOLD_TRANSLATION.md)

**What would upgrade it**
A proof-quality reduction showing that compactness dominance follows from exact
bounded-`k` structure under explicit hypotheses, or a broader maintained sweep
that survives additional cross-base and family stress tests without changing
the current threshold shape.

## 2. Cross-Base Hinge / Species Classification

**Maintained statement**
The repo now supports multiple short-length species rather than one universal
mechanism. Base `14` is the strongest current witness for overlap-led `M=2`
survival, while bases such as `34` point to a different boundary-led emergent
species.

**Constructive steelman**
The constructive version of the older “special bases” instinct is no longer
that one base is magically privileged. It is that some bases may sit at
different ecological niches in the short-length solution space: hinge bases,
boundary-release bases, and collapse bases. That is a smaller and more
plausible wonder than a universal base law.

**What we should not say yet**
We should not claim that all `2p` bases form one theorem-like family, or that
base `14` is universally special beyond the maintained boundary-layer evidence.

**Current evidence anchors**

- [`examples/two_p_family_report.rs`](../examples/two_p_family_report.rs)
- [`examples/two_p_hinge_report.rs`](../examples/two_p_hinge_report.rs)
- [`examples/two_p_hinge_mask_report.rs`](../examples/two_p_hinge_mask_report.rs)
- [`examples/base34_boundary_species_report.rs`](../examples/base34_boundary_species_report.rs)

**What would upgrade it**
An exact discriminator that explains why the overlap of persistence and
shared-yield core appears in base `14` but splits apart in `10`, `22`, `26`,
and `34`, preferably in unit-residue or mask-transfer language rather than by
case-by-case storytelling.

## 3. Connector Directional Asymmetry

**Maintained statement**
The connector lane remains a narrow but live research program: after exact
residue filtering, density-aware correction, and matched comparisons, some
connector families still show classifiable directional residuals, but the
current maintained evidence is taxonomic rather than universal.

**Constructive steelman**
The strongest version of the old Lagrange/connector romance that still seems
worth pursuing is not “prime balance points” as a global law. It is the more
modest idea that digit-constrained concatenation families may sort into stable
directional classes once the trivial congruence and density effects are peeled
away.

**What we should not say yet**
We should not present connector asymmetry as a general law of primes, and we
should not reuse midpoint/Lagrange metaphor language as if it were the current
mathematical object.

**Current evidence anchors**

- [`examples/connector_signal_report.rs`](../examples/connector_signal_report.rs)
- [`examples/comparative_signal_report.rs`](../examples/comparative_signal_report.rs)
- [`historical/examples/insertion_pair_taxonomy_scan.rs`](../historical/examples/insertion_pair_taxonomy_scan.rs)
- [`collab/CONNECTOR_SIGNAL.md`](./CONNECTOR_SIGNAL.md)

**What would upgrade it**
Broader same-method family coverage, successful cross-base stress tests, and a
smaller classifier that explains which connector families lean forward,
reverse, or flatten once the controlled residual is computed.

## 4. Formal Exact Substrate

**Maintained statement**
Lean and Agda now certify the exact bounded-`k` and connector-adjacent
combinatorics that make the current threshold and transfer stories legible:
compactness, admissibility masks, transfer buckets, exact overlap counts, and
curated finite witness catalogs.

**Constructive steelman**
If there is a deep mathematical payoff here, it probably will not first appear
as a sweeping empirical slogan. It will appear as exact finite grammar:
transfer structure, admissible overlap, compactness order, and the small
certified witnesses that keep the empirical language honest.

**What we should not say yet**
We should not claim that Lean or Agda prove the catalog-wide threshold, the
cross-base species language, or any density theorem. The formal systems prove
the substrate, not the full narrative.

**Current evidence anchors**

- [`lean-proofs/THEOREM_INDEX.md`](../lean-proofs/THEOREM_INDEX.md)
- [`collab/LEAN_THRESHOLD_SUBSTRATE.md`](./LEAN_THRESHOLD_SUBSTRATE.md)
- [`agda-proofs/STATUS.md`](../agda-proofs/STATUS.md)
- [`collab/AGDA_THRESHOLD_SUBSTRATE.md`](./AGDA_THRESHOLD_SUBSTRATE.md)

**What would upgrade it**
More arithmetic report language translated into exact finite statements,
especially where current overlap-led or boundary-led interpretations can be
restated without relying on percentages, labels, or prose-only species names.

## 5. Geometry / Visual Lane As Discovery Surface

**Maintained statement**
The visual lane is now a disciplined discovery surface rather than a source of
proof: unit-circle, residue-geometry, and 2D embedding views are useful when
they are treated as embeddings of exact arithmetic objects and are translated
back into transfer, overlap, or residue counts before any claim is promoted.

**Constructive steelman**
The repo may still be teaching us genuine structure through pictures. The
productive version of that hope is not that the complex plane or chord diagrams
contain hidden prime physics, but that carefully chosen geometric embeddings
can help us notice arithmetic species before we yet have the right theorem
language for them.

**What we should not say yet**
We should not treat complex-plane embeddings, chord atlases, or other 2D
visuals as theorem evidence on their own, and we should not imply that the
geometry itself proves a new mechanism.

**Current evidence anchors**

- [`examples/chaos_threshold_translation_report.rs`](../examples/chaos_threshold_translation_report.rs)
- [`scripts/plot_chaos_threshold_translation.py`](../scripts/plot_chaos_threshold_translation.py)
- [`collab/CHAOS_THRESHOLD_TRANSLATION.md`](./CHAOS_THRESHOLD_TRANSLATION.md)
- [/tmp/primes_chaos_threshold_translation_matplotlib/report.md](/tmp/primes_chaos_threshold_translation_matplotlib/report.md)

**What would upgrade it**
A repeated pattern where a visual cluster or geometric split is translated back
into exact arithmetic features and then survives quantitative checks across more
than one base or family.

## Practical Use

If repo prose needs to summarize “what still looks alive here,” prefer the
`Maintained statement` wording. If collaborator discussion needs room for
wonder, use the `Constructive steelman` language in the same breath as the
evidential boundary.

If planning effort, read the `Current Signal Ladder` first. It is the quickest
way to see where the repo has primary signal, explanatory signal, narrow
residual signal, rigor leverage, and discovery leverage.
