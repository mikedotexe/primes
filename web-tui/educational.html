What are Lagrange Points?

In space, these are positions where gravitational forces balance perfectly. In our prime universe, they represent mathematical equilibrium zones.

The Five Points:

  • L1 - Between the primes
  • L2 - Behind smaller prime
  • L3 - Behind larger prime
  • L4/L5 - Stable triangular points
